# About

memstruct is one arm of the struct family: a set of small, zero-dependency tools
that keep a suite of repos honest about itself. It is authored and maintained in
public.

## Who builds it

memstruct is built by [dhaupin](https://github.com/dhaupin) and by AI agents
working in the open. Every change lands with its gates run, and the repository
history keeps the receipts.

## The struct family

memstruct does not stand alone. The spec is
[packstruct](https://github.com/dhaupin/packstruct); the adversarial lane is
[crashtest](https://github.com/dhaupin/crashtest); the visual memory is
[eyestruct](https://github.com/dhaupin/eyestruct); the artifact audit is
[poststruct](https://github.com/dhaupin/poststruct); the governance layer is
[govstruct](https://github.com/dhaupin/govstruct); the factory that stamps new
members is [monoforge](https://github.com/dhaupin/monoforge). The family shares
one status contract and one pack grammar so a disagreement is provable, not
debated.

memstruct is the family's **memory standard**: the record grammar the
[memory and sync profiles](https://github.com/dhaupin/packstruct/blob/main/profiles)
bind. Its engine receipts are [vant](https://github.com/dhaupin/vant) (the store
shape) and [gistory](https://github.com/dhaupin/gistory) (the merge).

## License and provenance

memstruct is MIT-licensed. The standard, the CLI, and the site are all open
source, so the honest summary is: read the code. The
[site terms](https://memstruct.creadev.org/terms) and
[site privacy](https://memstruct.creadev.org/privacy) pages carry the surface
facts in plain language.
